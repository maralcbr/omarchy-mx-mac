Omarchy MX Mac

A complete Omarchy desktop for your Mac, powered by Arch Linux ARM and Asahi Linux.

Download the Mac app · ZIP

Developer ID signed · Apple notarized · No PKG required

Install from macOS

  1. Download and extract the ZIP.
  2. Open Omarchy MX Mac Installer.app directly from Downloads.
  3. Review your disk allocation, authorize installation, and follow the app’s Recovery instructions.

The app defaults to RC and checks the selected channel’s latest signed release. Matching cached files are verified and reused. The familiar installation screens stay the same.

Back up your data and keep your Mac connected to power. Hardware support depends on your model’s Asahi Linux support.

End-to-end installation with the portable app was confirmed by the owner on an M2 Max. Diagnostic logs survive reboot; credentials are never logged.
